\begin{abstract}
Verifier-guided search, process reward models and hallucination-repair loops share one assumption:
a failing generation goes wrong at an identifiable step, and resampling from just before that step
is better than starting over. We test this assumption end to end, on mathematical reasoning and on
retrieval-augmented generation, with a single quantity: $v_k$, the generator's own probability of
success from prefix $k$, measured by on-policy resampling.

\emph{Detection.} On 3,400 ProcessBench traces, early-warning detectors mostly read difficulty:
question and generator alone reach AUROC \aucQG, and a quarter of the reasoning adds \dIncA\ (n.s.).

\emph{Commitment.} From \TotalRollouts\ continuations of four open models, value curves replicate
across independent batches ($r=\QwenSmallAllSplitR$). \QwenSmallAllCapPct--\QwenMathCapPct\% of
failures are decided before the first step. When the outcome is still open, the human-labelled
first error is where success collapses in \QwenTwoAllDec--\QwenSmallAllDec\ of traces (null
$\approx$0.18; preregistered and confirmed on a held-out model family).

\emph{Localisation.} We release ChokeBench, which grades step localisers on both targets. A
validated process reward model finds the human first error in \cbPrmDropAccHPct\% of open
failures but the choke point in only \cbPrmDropAccDPct\%.

\emph{Recovery.} No deployable resampling point reliably beats restart. At a matched end-to-end budget, PRM-guided
backtracking is worse than restart (\LlamaPolPrmDropFourDiff\ on the preregistered model), and even
an oracle that knows the choke point for free gains only \cbOracleGainB\ [\cbOracleGainBLo,
\cbOracleGainBHi] at four restarts' budget. A supermartingale argument explains why. In RAG
(RAGTruth, Llama-2-7B), a preregistered commitment-aware controller is equivalent to restarting
until the detector passes, and its choice of where to resample adds \rrHFive\
[\rrHFiveLo, \rrHFiveHi]. We also find that the apparent size of such recovery gains depends on
the evaluator: \robBaseETwoDiff\ under an evaluator sharing the gate detector's training data,
\robBaseEOneDiff\ (n.s.) under an independent judge.

Across both domains, restart is the baseline that local recovery must beat at matched compute,
and it rarely is. All rollouts, preregistrations and the code behind every number are released.
\end{abstract}
